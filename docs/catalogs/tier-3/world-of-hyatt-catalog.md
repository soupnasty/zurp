## Benefit Catalog

### Points & Earning Structure

```benefit_key: earning_structure_hyatt
4x points on all purchases at Hyatt properties including room rates, food and beverage, and resort incidentals
2x points on dining, airline tickets, and eligible transit (public transportation, taxis, ride-sharing)
2x points on fitness club and gym memberships
1x point on all other purchases, including groceries and car rentals
```

```benefit_key: dining_scope_hyatt
Dining earning includes restaurants, bars, and coffee shops worldwide. Food delivery services earn the 1x base rate.
```

### Elite Status & Benefits

```benefit_key: automatic_discoverist_elite
Automatic Discoverist Elite status with World of Hyatt loyalty program, providing room upgrades (upon availability), breakfast benefit, late checkout, and priority customer service.
```

### Free Night Certificates

```benefit_key: free_night_certificate_annual
Annual free night certificate valid at any World of Hyatt property in Category 1-4 (limited to suite availability and applicable terms). Certificate expires December 31st of the following year.
```

```benefit_key: second_fnc_15k_spend
Additional free night certificate in the same Category 1-4 range earned after $15,000 in card spending within a calendar year.
```

```benefit_key: fnc_eligibility_notes
Free night certificates are suite-eligible at select properties. Standard room rate availability may be lower than published. Certificates do not provide elite qualifying nights.
```

### Tier-Qualifying Night Credits

```benefit_key: tier_qualifying_nights_bonus
Earn 2 tier-qualifying night credits toward elite status for every $5,000 in card spending. Accelerates path to higher elite tiers within World of Hyatt.
```

### Chase Ultimate Rewards Transfer

```benefit_key: chase_ur_transfer_partnership
Chase Ultimate Rewards points transfer 1:1 directly to World of Hyatt points, enabling flexibility and transferability across the broader Chase rewards ecosystem.
```

### Visa Signature Benefits

```benefit_key: visa_signature_protections
Visa Signature benefits include travel accident insurance, emergency evacuation coverage, and standard purchase protections.
```

### International Features

```benefit_key: no_foreign_transaction_fee
No foreign transaction fees apply to international purchases, ideal for frequent Hyatt travelers to global properties.
```

### Absent Premium Benefits

```benefit_key: no_resort_credit
Unlike premium hotel cards, World of Hyatt does not include annual resort or incidental credits.
```

```benefit_key: no_lounge_access
Lounge access benefits (Priority Pass, hotel club lounges) are not included except through elite status.
```

```benefit_key: no_airline_credit
No airline credit or incidental airline benefits included beyond the 2x earning on airline tickets.
```

## Zurp Relevance

The World of Hyatt Chase represents the mid-tier hotel loyalty card in the Chase premium ecosystem, delivering strong value for committed Hyatt frequent travelers without requiring the elevated annual fees of luxury alternatives. The $95 annual fee is reasonable given the annual free night certificate (Categories 1-4) and potential second FNC at $15K spend. The 4x earning on Hyatt properties combined with 2x on dining, flights, and transit aligns well with the typical Hyatt traveler demographic. The integration with Chase Ultimate Rewards allows points flexibility and transferability to other Chase transfer partners, creating optionality absent in single-brand-only cards. Tier-qualifying night credits accelerate elite progression for frequent spenders. The card's primary limitation is the lower free night certificate cap (Category 1-4) compared to premium competitors and the absence of annual resort or airline credits. Best suited for Hyatt loyalists with annual Hyatt spending exceeding $10,000 and who appreciate the flexibility of the Chase rewards ecosystem.
