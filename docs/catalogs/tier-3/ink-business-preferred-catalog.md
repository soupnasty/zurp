## Benefit Catalog


```benefit_key: earning_shipping_category
3x Ultimate Rewards points per $1 spent on shipping services including UPS, FedEx, USPS, and other courier services used for business shipping and logistics.
```

```benefit_key: earning_internet_phone_category
3x Ultimate Rewards points per $1 spent on internet and phone services including broadband, wireless services, and telecommunications expenses for business operations.
```

```benefit_key: earning_advertising_category
3x Ultimate Rewards points per $1 spent on advertising and marketing services including Google Ads, Facebook Ads, digital marketing platforms, and traditional advertising services.
```

```benefit_key: earning_lyft_rides
5x Ultimate Rewards points per $1 spent on Lyft ride-sharing services through September 2027, providing accelerated earning on commute and business ground transportation.
```

```benefit_key: earning_combined_category_cap
The combined 3x earning on travel, shipping, internet, phone, and advertising is capped at $150,000 per year in combined spending, meaning only $150,000 in these categories earns 3x annually. Spending above this cap earns 1x.
```

```benefit_key: earning_other_purchases
1x Ultimate Rewards point per $1 spent on all other eligible purchases not listed in the premium earning categories, establishing baseline earning across business expenses.
```

## Detailed Benefit Analysis

The Chase Ink Business Preferred represents an excellent option for small business owners and entrepreneurs who can optimize spending across the 3x earning categories. The $95 annual fee is among the lowest in the business card category and is rapidly offset by the 100,000 point welcome bonus (worth approximately $1,000-$2,000 depending on redemption method).

The card's core strength lies in its versatility across common business expense categories. The 3x earning on travel, shipping, internet/phone, and advertising addresses four critical small business spending categories simultaneously. For example, a business spending $100,000 across these categories would earn 300,000 points annually, far exceeding the welcome bonus value. The $150,000 annual cap on combined 3x earning is generous for many small businesses but may underperform for high-volume businesses with significant category spending.

The 5x Lyft earning extends through September 2027, providing accelerated earning on ride-sharing and commute expenses during a multi-year promotional window. This temporary multiplier should influence card selection timing, as the return period is defined.

The Ultimate Rewards ecosystem stands out as the primary differentiator: point pooling with other Sapphire cards allows household members to consolidate points for strategic redemptions, and the 14+ transfer partners at 1:1 rate provide flexibility approaching premium card programs. The ability to transfer points to United, Southwest, Delta, American, and Marriott creates premium redemption opportunities.

Weaknesses include the $150,000 annual category cap, which limits earning velocity for high-spend businesses (though unusual for most small operations), and the absence of business-specific perks like purchasing power discounts or merchant partnerships. The card also lacks employee cardholder provisioning, limiting enterprise suitability for larger organizations.

Best suited for small business owners with annual spending of $30,000-$150,000 across the earning categories who value point flexibility and transfer partner options. The card deteriorates for businesses with minimal travel/shipping spending or those requiring employee card programs.
