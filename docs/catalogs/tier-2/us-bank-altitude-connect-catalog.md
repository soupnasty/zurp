## Benefit Catalog

### Points & Earning Structure

```benefit_key: earning_structure_altitude_connect
5x points on prepaid hotels and rental cars booked through the US Bank Rewards Center
4x points on travel (excluding prepaid hotels/cars), gas, and electric vehicle charging stations (capped at $1,000 per quarter, then 1x)
2x points on dining, groceries, and streaming services
1x point on all other purchases
```

```benefit_key: exclusions_grocery_2x
Discount stores, supercenters, and warehouse clubs are excluded from the 2x grocery bonus. Only purchases at traditional grocers qualify.
```

### Travel & Lounge Benefits

```benefit_key: priority_pass_alternative
Priority Pass Select membership with 4 free airport lounge visits per year. Additional visits incur standard fees.
```

```benefit_key: global_entry_tsa_precheck
Up to $100 credit toward Global Entry, TSA PreCheck, or trusted traveler programs, provided once every 4 years.
```

### Trip Protection Coverage

```benefit_key: trip_cancellation_coverage
Trip cancellation protection provides reimbursement up to $2,000 per person if your prepaid trip is canceled for a covered reason.
```

```benefit_key: trip_delay_coverage
Trip delay reimbursement up to $300 if your trip is delayed more than 12 consecutive hours on a Common Carrier.
```

```benefit_key: lost_luggage_coverage
Lost luggage protection reimburses essential items up to $3,000 per trip if your luggage is temporarily lost or delayed.
```

```benefit_key: travel_accident_insurance
Travel accident insurance provides coverage up to $500,000 in case of accidental death or dismemberment while traveling on a Common Carrier.
```

### Redemption & Servicing

```benefit_key: real_time_rewards
Real-Time Rewards allows cardholders to redeem points instantly via text message for cash back or travel purchases without waiting for billing cycles.
```

### Discontinued/Removed Benefits

```benefit_key: streaming_credit_discontinued
Streaming credit benefit discontinued. No longer available to new or existing cardholders.
```

```benefit_key: cell_phone_protection_discontinued
Cell phone protection benefit discontinued. No longer available to new or existing cardholders.
```

### Absent Protections

```benefit_key: no_purchase_protection
Purchase protection/return protection is not included with this card.
```

```benefit_key: no_extended_warranty
Extended warranty protection is not included with this card.
```

```benefit_key: no_rental_car_damage
Rental car damage waiver (CDW) is not included. User must arrange separate coverage for rental vehicles.
```

### International Features

```benefit_key: no_foreign_transaction_fee
No foreign transaction fees apply to purchases made outside the United States.
```

## Zurp Relevance

The US Bank Altitude Connect serves as a strong value-tier, no-annual-fee alternative in the travel rewards ecosystem. The elimination of the $95 annual fee in September 2024 significantly enhanced its competitive positioning against premium cards. The card's strength lies in its flexible 5x earning on prepaid bookings through the Rewards Center (uncapped) and comprehensive trip protection suite. However, the absence of purchase protection, extended warranty, and rental car damage waiver limits its appeal for business travelers requiring full protection coverage. The discontinuation of streaming and cell phone credits further narrowed the card's lifestyle benefits. Ideal for cost-conscious travelers seeking straightforward points accumulation without premium fees.
