## When to Keep vs. Close

**Keep if:**
- Annual travel spending exceeds $20,000
- Book hotels regularly via Citi Travel portal
- Take 4+ flights per year
- Frequent international travel (0% FTF critical)
- Want premium transfer partner network
- Need primary international car rental CDW

**Close if:**
- Annual travel spending < $5,000
- Primarily domestic travel (CSR better value)
- Rarely book hotels (lose $100 annual credit benefit)
- Don't value transfer partner flexibility
- Prefer flat-rate card simplicity (Venture X)
- 0% FTF not important

### Optimization Strategy

1. **Maximize Portal Bookings:** Book hotels through Citi Travel for 10x points; earn more than annual fee
2. **Use Annual Hotel Credit:** Plan $500+ hotel booking to capture $100 annual credit
3. **Flight Priority:** Use for all flight bookings (3x points)
4. **International Travel:** Leverage 0% FTF; avoid other cards when traveling abroad
5. **Transfer Strategy:** Analyze transfer partner options; combine with other Citi cards for pooling
6. **Annual Justification:** Ensure $20K+ annual travel spending to justify $95 fee
7. **Comparison:** Against CSR for US travel, Amex Platinum for aspirational, or Capital One Venture X for flat rates
