01
The logomark
A credit card containing two stacked bars. The color segments mirror — forming a hidden Z through the blue diagonal.
What it communicates
Credit card — the rounded rectangle outline. Comparison — two bars, one wins (bright), one loses (dim). The formula — blue (points) + purple (benefits) − red (fees). The brand initial — Z traced through the blue diagonal. 
Z reads through blue:
  ████████████████████████████   blue anchors top-left
                  ╲
  ████████████████████████████   blue anchors bottom-right

Construction rules
Bars are seamless — no gaps between segments. Clipped to a single rounded rect.
Bottom bar at 50% opacity — the "losing" card is dimmer.
Blue segments are equal width (18px each) — symmetry makes the Z readable.
Bar gap = 4px — top ends at y=15, bottom starts at y=19.
Card fill = #0a0e17 — not transparent. Bars float on dark interior.

07
Usage rules
Guidelines for consistent use across all contexts.

Do

  • Use F3 (dark fill, seamless) as the primary mark
  • Maintain the Z — bottom bar stays flipped
  • Scale proportionally, never stretch
  • Use full lockup when space allows
  • Use mark alone for app icons and favicons
  • Increase stroke-width at small sizes

Don't

  • Rearrange segment colors or proportions
  • Use without dark card fill on dark backgrounds
  • Add drop shadows or outer glows
  • Animate the nav or footer logo
  • Place on busy or photographic backgrounds
  • Lock up wordmark above or below the mark
