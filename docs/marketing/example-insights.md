# Example Insights for Landing Page

4 examples that showcase Zurp's most impactful insight types. Each represents a distinct engine capability and a clear dollar-value payoff users can immediately understand.

---

## 1. A2 — Subscription Swap

**Type label:** Subscription swap
**Variant:** blue (redirect)

> You're paying **$15.99/mo for Uber Eats** but your card includes **$25/mo** in DoorDash credits. Switch your deliveries to DoorDash to save **$192/yr**.

| Savings value | Savings label | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| $192 | per year by switching | Chase Sapphire Reserve · DoorDash credit |

**Why this one:** Immediately relatable — almost everyone has a food delivery subscription. The swap is dead simple (one app to another), the math is obvious, and $192/yr is a concrete number that sticks. This is the "aha moment" insight.

---

## 2. B1 — Credit Expiring (Urgent)

**Type label:** Credit expiring
**Variant:** amber (urgency)

> Your **$50 Saks credit** resets in **4 days** and you haven't used any of it. That's $50 gone — even a small purchase counts.

| Savings value | Savings label | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| $50 | expiring in 4 days | Amex Platinum · Semi-annual credit |

**Why this one:** Creates urgency without being generic. Users immediately feel the cost of inaction. Saks credit is one of the most commonly forgotten Amex Platinum benefits — high recognition factor.

---

## 3. A3 — Wrong Card for Category

**Type label:** Earning opportunity
**Variant:** blue (redirect)

> You spent **$4,200 on dining** this year. The **Amex Gold** earns **4x** vs your **1x** — that's **~$126 more** in points value.

| Savings value | Savings label | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| $126 | more per year on dining | Amex Platinum · Cross-card comparison |

**Why this one:** This is Zurp's most sophisticated insight — it compares across cards the user doesn't even have. It plants the seed for card strategy without being pushy. Dining is the highest-frequency spending category, so the numbers are always meaningful.

---

## 4. P2 — Portal Redirect

**Type label:** Booking redirect
**Variant:** blue (redirect)

> You booked **$1,800 in hotels** direct this year. Book through **Chase Travel Portal** for **10x** instead of **3x** — that's **~$126 more** in points.

| Savings value | Savings label | Meta |
|---|---|---|
| $126 | more per year via portal | Chase Sapphire Reserve · Travel portal |

**Why this one:** Travel portal redirects are one of the highest-value optimizations in credit card strategy, yet most people don't know about them. The 10x vs 3x contrast is visually dramatic and the dollar value is substantial.
