**Card Slug:** `chase-sapphire-reserve`

## 1. Travel Credit

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **ID** | `travel-credit` |
| **Display Name** | Travel Credit |
| **Annual Value** | $300 |
| **Schedule** | Anniversary year (resets on your cardmember anniversary date) |
| **Carryover** | No — unused balance resets on anniversary |
| **Expires** | Ongoing (core card benefit, no end date) |
| **Activation Required** | No |

**Description:**
Up to $300 in automatic statement credits each cardmember anniversary year as reimbursement for travel purchases charged to your card. This is the most flexible travel credit available — there's no need to book through Chase Travel.

**How to Use:**
Use your Sapphire Reserve to pay for any purchase that codes as travel. This includes flights, hotels, car rentals, taxis, rideshares, tolls, parking, campgrounds, cruises, and more. The credit posts automatically — no activation or portal booking required. Qualifying purchases will not earn points on the portion covered by the credit.

**Links:**
- Learn More: https://account.chase.com/sapphire/reserve/benefits
- Chase Travel Portal: https://www.chase.com/travel

**Transaction Matching Notes:**
Matches any transaction categorized as "Travel" by Chase. Broad category includes airlines, hotels, car rentals, taxis/rideshares, parking, tolls, trains, buses, cruises, travel agencies, campgrounds, and timeshares.

## 2. The Edit Hotel Credit

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **ID** | `edit-hotel-credit` |
| **Display Name** | The Edit Hotel Credit |
| **Annual Value** | $500 ($250 × 2 semi-annual periods) |
| **Schedule** | Semi-annual: Jan 1 – Jun 30 ($250 max) and Jul 1 – Dec 31 ($250 max) |
| **Carryover** | No — unused portion does not carry between periods |
| **Expires** | Ongoing (core card benefit) |
| **Activation Required** | No |

**Description:**
Up to $500 in annual statement credits for prepaid hotel stays booked through The Edit by Chase Travel — a curated collection of 1,100+ hand-picked luxury hotels and resorts. Starting January 1, 2026, cardmembers receive up to $250 per transaction (previously split biannually). Two-night minimum stay required.

**How to Use:**
1. Go to Chase Travel (chase.com/travel or the Chase app)
2. Search for hotels and look for properties marked with "The Edit" badge
3. Book a prepaid stay of at least 2 nights using your Sapphire Reserve
4. The statement credit posts automatically after the charge

The Edit stays also include complimentary perks: $100 property credit, daily breakfast for two, room upgrade (if available), and more. Purchases that qualify for the credit will not earn points.

**Links:**
- Book via Chase Travel: https://www.chase.com/travel
- The Edit Collection: https://account.chase.com/sapphire/reserve/benefits
- Learn More: https://account.chase.com/sapphire/reserve/benefits

**Transaction Matching Notes:**
Only matches prepaid hotel bookings made through The Edit on Chase Travel. Direct hotel bookings or non-Edit Chase Travel bookings do not qualify. Look for Chase Travel merchant name on the transaction.

Up to $300 in annual statement credits when dining at restaurants participating in the Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables program. The program provides primetime reservations at 275+ curated restaurants in major U.S. cities through the Visa Dining Collection on OpenTable.

| **ID** | `doordash-credits` |
| **Display Name** | DoorDash Credits |
| **Annual Value** | $300 in promos + $120 DashPass membership value |
| **Schedule** | Monthly: $5 restaurant promo + two $10 non-restaurant promos = $25/month |
| **Carryover** | No — monthly promos expire at end of each calendar month, cannot be stacked |
| **Expires** | December 31, 2027 (DashPass and promos) |
| **Activation Required** | **Yes** — activate DashPass on DoorDash by 12/31/2027 |

**Description:**
Complimentary DashPass membership ($0 delivery fee, lower service fees) plus up to $25/month in DoorDash promos: one $5 promo on a restaurant order and two $10 promos on non-restaurant orders (grocery, convenience, retail). If the full promo value isn't used on a single order, the remaining value is forfeited for that promo.

**How to Use:**
1. Download or open the DoorDash app
2. Add your Sapphire Reserve as the default payment method
3. Follow the prompts to activate your complimentary DashPass membership
4. Each month, select the promo from your promotion wallet at checkout
5. For non-restaurant promos: filter by "Pickup" → "Grocery" or "Convenience" for best value
6. Make sure the "Chase monthly benefit" toggle is ON at checkout

**Links:**
- Activate DashPass: https://www.doordash.com/dashpass/partner/chase/sapphire-reserve
- DoorDash App: https://www.doordash.com
- Terms: https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/offer-terms-conditions?language=en_US
- Learn More: https://www.doordash.com/dashpass/partner/chase/sapphire-reserve

**Transaction Matching Notes:**
DoorDash promos are in-app discounts, not statement credits — they won't appear as Chase credits on the statement. Track by looking for DoorDash transactions where promo was applied (the order total will be reduced). The DashPass membership value ($120/yr) is the avoided subscription fee, not a trackable transaction.

## 9. Global Entry / TSA PreCheck / NEXUS

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **ID** | `global-entry` |
| **Display Name** | Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit |
| **Value** | Up to $120 every 4 years |
| **Schedule** | Once every 4 years |
| **Carryover** | N/A — one-time use per 4-year cycle |
| **Expires** | Ongoing (core card benefit, no end date) |
| **Activation Required** | No — just pay the application fee with your CSR |

**Description:**
One statement credit of up to $120 every four years to reimburse the application fee for Global Entry ($120), TSA PreCheck ($78–$98), or NEXUS ($50). Only one program per 4-year cycle. The credit covers the primary cardmember or an authorized user.

**How to Use:**
1. Apply for Global Entry, TSA PreCheck, or NEXUS through the official government site
2. Pay the application fee with your Sapphire Reserve card
3. The statement credit posts automatically within 1–2 billing cycles
4. No activation needed — just use your CSR for payment

Only purchases made directly through the Department of Homeland Security, an eligible TTP partner, or an authorized TSA PreCheck enrollment provider qualify. Third-party services and travel agencies are NOT eligible.

**Links:**
- Global Entry Application: https://ttp.cbp.dhs.gov/
- TSA PreCheck Application: https://www.tsa.gov/precheck
- NEXUS: https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/travel-voyage/prog/nexus/menu-eng.html
- Learn More: https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/sapphire-reserve-tsa-precheck-global-entry

**Transaction Matching Notes:**
Matches a single transaction from U.S. Department of Homeland Security, CBP, or authorized TSA PreCheck enrollment provider. One credit per 4-year cycle. After redemption, this benefit should show as "Used" until the next 4-year window.
